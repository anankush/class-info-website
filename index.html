About Us

Welcome to the official class information page of B.Tech CSE (Cyber Security) at Budge Budge Institute of Technology, affiliated to Maulana Abul Kalam Azad University of Technology (MAKAUT), West Bengal. We are students from the 2022-2023 academic batch, currently pursuing our degree under the Department of Computer Science and Engineering.

Our curriculum is designed to build a strong foundation in both core computer science and specialized cyber security domains. From Data Structures to Cyber Law, our coursework covers a wide range of topics essential for modern IT professionals.
